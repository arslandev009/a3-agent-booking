# Clinic FAQ (fictional: BrightSmile Dental, sample data only)

## Opening hours
We are open Monday to Saturday, 09:00 to 18:00. We close for lunch from 13:00 to 14:00. We are closed on Sundays and public holidays.

## Location and parking
BrightSmile Dental is on the first floor of the Example Plaza, Main Boulevard (fictional address). Free parking is available behind the building.

## Services and duration
Checkup 30 minutes, cleaning 30 minutes, filling 60 minutes, root canal consultation 30 minutes, whitening consultation 30 minutes.

## Consultation fees
A checkup costs PKR 2,000. A cleaning costs PKR 4,000. Filling prices depend on the tooth and are confirmed by the dentist after the exam. Prices are indicative sample data.

## Payment methods
We accept cash, bank transfer and major debit or credit cards.

## Cancellation and refund policy
Appointments can be cancelled up to 24 hours before the visit. Cancellation requests are confirmed by clinic staff. Refunds of deposits are handled by staff only and cannot be promised by the assistant.

## First visit
Please arrive 10 minutes early and bring a photo ID. Previous dental X-rays are helpful but not required.

## Emergencies
For severe pain with facial swelling, trouble breathing or swallowing, bleeding that will not stop, or a knocked-out tooth, call the clinic emergency line or go to the nearest emergency room. The assistant cannot give medical advice.

## Insurance
We can provide a receipt for insurance claims. Direct billing is not available.
